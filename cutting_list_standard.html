Workshop Notes — Standard Build

Board Formats: The 2800mm Desktop (A1) and 2800mm Cable Lid (A2) require 1× 3050×1220mm board (trimmed to 2800mm). Both parts fit on this single sheet. All other structural panels fit on standard 2440×1220mm boards.
Grain Continuity: Oak veneer grain runs along the length of each board. On the desktop and lid, grain flows continuously along the 2800mm length.
19mm Panel Processing: 19mm Oak-Veneered MDF provides substantial rigidity while reducing overall desk weight compared to 30mm. Always use a sacrificial backing board or scoring blade to avoid surface veneer chip-out.
Cable Trench Routing: Route the 100mm trench channel using a guide rail from the underside to guarantee zero top-veneer chip-out.
Trench Floor Cable Pass-Throughs: The trench floor and sub-top cable tray cannot be solid; it must feature routed pass-through apertures (12× 25×60mm capsule slots or 60–80mm circular grommet holes) aligned directly above the Tower PC compartment and 19" equipment rack, allowing all sub-desk wiring to rise directly into the rear cable trough.
Joinery Pre-Drilling: Pilot-drill all screw holes at 3.5mm and countersink cleanly to prevent core swelling.
Off-Cut Reclamation: The remaining strips on Sheet 1 (214mm wide × 3050mm long) provide excellent material for structural cleats and cable duct supports.

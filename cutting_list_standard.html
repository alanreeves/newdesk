Board Layout — Optimised Nesting

Parts nested onto 2440×1220mm standard boards using guillotine bin-packing. 3mm saw-kerf between cuts. Hover a part to see its label and size. Note: 30mm boards are non-standard — 25mm or 32mm nearest stock sizes. Confirm availability with your supplier.

◆Drawer Runners: 4 Pairs×600mm Soft-Close Ball Bearing Slides (side-mount, 45kg).
◆PC Extension Slides: 1 Pair×750mm Heavy Extension Slides (45kg+ rated).
◆Drawer Pull Handles: 4×Bar Pulls (120mm hole centres).

◆Pocket Screws: ~200×50mm for panel joints (longer than standard due to 30mm thickness).
◆Fixing Bolts: 8×M6×70mm barrel nuts & bolts, desktop to carcass (longer for 30mm panels).
◆Wood Glue: 500ml PVA at all glued joints. Clamp 60 min minimum.

Build Notes

Board Thickness: 30mm solid oak MDF is non-standard. Closest stock sizes are 25mm and 32mm — check with your timber merchant and adjust panel positions accordingly.
Grain Direction: Oak panels should have grain running along the longest dimension for visual continuity across the 3000mm desk front.
Desktop Machining: Route the 100mm cable trench slot from the underside to prevent surface tear-out. Use a straight-edge fence jig the full 3000mm length.
Panel Weight: At 30mm, the desktop panel alone weighs approx. 65kg. Plan for two or more people for installation and a mechanically-fixed support system.
Joinery: Pocket screws should be 50mm for 30mm panels. Consider adding Domino or loose-tenon joints at all visible panel-to-panel faces for extra rigidity.
Edge Banding: Use 2mm thick ABS edge banding for 30mm panels. Apply with a hot-air bander, flush-trim, then 240-grit sand before fitting.
Structural Benefit: 30mm panels provide a significantly stiffer, heavier carcass. All joints must be pre-drilled to prevent splitting; especially at end-grain faces.
Off-Cuts: Reserve substantial off-cuts for support battens, cleat fixing strips, and drawer kicker rails (rip to 30×30mm or 30×50mm sections).

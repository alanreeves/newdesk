Hardware Schedule

  • Piano Hinge: 1× 2730mm stainless steel continuous hinge (rear edge of 150mm lid).
  • Trench Divider: 1× 2730mm (or 2× 1365mm) × 48mm × 1.5–2mm aluminium sheet.
  • Floor Hatches: 2× liftable plug-access panels (PC & rack bays) with finger pulls.
  • Slides: 3 pairs 600mm soft-close drawer slides (35kg) + 1 pair 750mm PC slides (45kg+).
  • Handles & Grommets: 3× black bar pulls (120mm) + 24× 25×60mm rubber grommets.
  • Fixings: 50mm Confirmat / 4×45mm screws, 8× M6×50mm bolts, PVA glue, ~55m 2mm ABS.

Workshop & Cable Segregation Notes

  • Grain Direction: Run continuous grain along 2730mm length on desktop (A1) & lid (A2).
  • 150mm Dual-Channel Trench: Machine 3–4mm × 6mm groove along trench floor centreline (75mm) for aluminium divider — Rear 75mm = 230V AC Mains, Front 75mm = Clean Audio/Data.
  • Zero-Chip MFC Cutting: Use 80T triple-chip grind (TCG) blade or score cut lines first.
  • Pilot Drilling: Pre-drill all MFC screw holes with 3.5mm bit and countersink cleanly.
  • Board Nesting: All 38 pieces cut from 3× 2800×2070mm boards (see Nesting Diagrams).

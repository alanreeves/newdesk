  • Edge Banding: ~45 metres of 2mm Light Oak ABS Edging + ~30 metres of 1mm Light Oak Edging.

6. Joinery Notes & Workshop Instructions

  • Grain Direction: All visible faces (Desktop D1, Cable Lid D2, Gables V1-V7, and Drawer Fronts DF1) must have wood grain running horizontally along the 3000mm length for unbroken visual alignment.
  • Desktop Profile: Straight front edge with 2mm Light Oak ABS edge banding applied to all four outer edges.
  • Ventilation: 80mm circular cutouts to be drilled through rear section of center PC & amp bay for active air cooling.

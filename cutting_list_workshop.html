Workshop Notes — Workshop Build

Board Formats: The 3000mm Desktop (A1) and 3000mm Cable Lid (A2) require 1× 3050×1220mm board. Both parts fit on this single sheet. All other oak parts fit on 2× 2440×1220mm boards; black melamine fits on 3× 2440×1220mm boards; birch fits on 1× 2440×1220mm board.
Grain Direction: Oak-veneered panels must have grain oriented along the length. Desktop and lid grain flow continuously along the 3000mm length.
Black Melamine Machining: Melamine is prone to chipping. Score cut lines lightly with a utility knife before cutting, or use a scoring blade / 80T triple-chip blade.
Cable Trench Routing: Route the 100mm trench channel using a guide rail from the underside to guarantee zero top-veneer chip-out.
Carcass Rigidity: 18mm panels provide excellent rigidity when glued and pocket-screwed. The 3000mm top bears on all 7 vertical dividers.
Edge Banding Application: Apply 1mm ABS edge banding with a hot-melt edge bander or iron. Trim flush with edge trimmer and break sharpness with 240-grit block.

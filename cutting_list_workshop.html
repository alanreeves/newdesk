Board Layout — Optimised Nesting

Parts nested onto 2440×1220mm boards using a guillotine bin-packing algorithm. 3mm saw-kerf between cuts. Hover a part to see its label and size.

Hardware Schedule

◆Trench Lid Hinge: 1×3000mm Continuous Piano Hinge, stainless steel, heavy-duty.
◆Drawer Runners: 4 Pairs×600mm Soft-Close Ball Bearing Slides (side-mount, 35kg).
◆PC Extension Slides: 1 Pair×750mm Heavy Extension Slides (45kg+ rated).
◆Drawer Pull Handles: 4×Matt Black Bar Pulls (120mm hole centres).
◆Cable Grommets: 24×Black Rubber Capsule Grommets (25×60mm cut-out).
◆Oak ABS Edge Banding: ~18m of 1mm Oak ABS — all exposed oak-veneer edges.
◆Black ABS Edge Banding: ~28m of 1mm Black ABS — all exposed melamine edges.
◆Pocket Screws: ~200×32mm for panel joints. Domino or biscuits at visible faces.
◆Fixing Bolts: 8×M6×50mm barrel nuts & bolts, desktop to carcass.
◆Wood Glue: 500ml PVA at all glued joints. Clamp 60 min minimum.

Workshop Notes

Nominal Dimensions: All dimensions are nominal. Deduct 18mm at joints when marking out — e.g. shelf width = bay width − 2×18mm.
Grain Direction: Oak-veneered panels must have grain along the longest dimension for visual continuity across the 3000mm desk front.
Desktop Machining: Route the 100mm cable trench from the underside to avoid veneer tear-out. Use a straight-edge fence jig across the full 3000mm.
Structural Note: 18mm is adequate when properly glued and fastened. The 3000mm desktop must bear on all seven vertical dividers to prevent sag.
Black Melamine Cutting: Score cut lines with a knife before sawing. Use an 80T+ fine-tooth blade and slow feed to prevent chipping the face.
Edge Banding: Apply ABS with a hot-air bander or iron. Flush-trim with a router then 240-grit sand before fitting.
Verify on Site: Confirm room/door allows the 3000mm desk to manoeuvre in. Longest dimension ~3020mm with banding.
Off-Cuts: Reserve strips for fixing cleats, kicker rails and battens (typically 18×18mm or 18×40mm ripped sections).
